# Privacy Policy – Cal

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Cal is a calendar app developed by Simon Luerwer. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

## The short version
Cal does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device and in your own calendar accounts.

## Calendar data
If you allow calendar access, Cal reads and edits the events in the calendars on your device (for example iCloud) so it can show them and save the changes you make. This data is handled by iOS and synced only through your own calendar accounts. Cal never sends it to us or to anyone else.

## Widget
The Cal home screen widget shows your upcoming events. It reads them on your device in the same way as the app.

## Notifications
If you allow notifications, Cal schedules reminders (for example for birthdays) locally on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

## Settings
Your app preferences (such as colors, default calendar and notification times) are stored only on your device.

## No tracking, no analytics, no third parties
Cal contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not share data with third parties. Cal has no servers and does not require an account.

## Beta testing (TestFlight)
If you test Cal through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and usage information according to Apple's own privacy policy. Developers can see this information only in anonymized form, or as feedback you choose to send.

## Children
Cal is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
